## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **WORKSPACE** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding the privacy, integrity, and security of all personal, institutional, and student data processed through our mobile application. This Privacy Policy details how WORKSPACE collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when school directors, principals, administrators, and authorized management personnel utilize the application.

WORKSPACE is an enterprise administrative command center designed exclusively for authorized executives and staff of educational institutions subscribed to **Nepal School Hub (NSHLMS)**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information Provided by You and Your Educational Institution
To deliver administrative management, operational oversight, and institutional decision-making capabilities, the app processes:
* **Administrator Profile & Credentials:** Full name, administrative role/designation, official email address, mobile number, and encrypted authentication credentials.
* **Institutional & Financial Metrics:** Aggregate fee collections, cashflow balances, fee concession approvals, expense vouchers, payroll summaries, and daily cashier register summaries.
* **Student & Academic Records:** Student roster data, roll numbers, class/section assignments, attendance logs, academic evaluation reports, emergency contact details, and parent/guardian contact numbers.
* **Staff & HR Records:** Staff attendance logs, leave requests, duty rosters, substitute assignments, and official designations.
* **Operational Documents & Media:** Circulars, identity photographs, invoices, official letters, and admission documents uploaded by authorized administrators.

### B. Device & Technical Information
* **Device Identifiers & Hardware Details:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network connection status.
* **Security & Authentication Logs:** IP addresses, login timestamps, security lock events, and device session tokens to prevent unauthorized administrative access.
* **Diagnostic Data:** Crash logs, API response timings, and performance metrics utilized strictly for app stability.

---

## 3. Device Permissions & Purpose

The app requests only hardware and system permissions strictly essential for administrative tasks:

| Permission | Purpose & Justification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE** | Required for secure HTTPS/TLS data synchronization between the app and the school's central cloud database. |
| **CAMERA** | Required for the integrated QR/barcode scanner (`mobile_scanner`) used at school gates or events to verify student/staff ID cards, and to capture images of official vouchers or receipts. |
| **READ / WRITE EXTERNAL STORAGE / PHOTOS** | Required for uploading institutional documents, circulars, fee receipts, or exporting analytics reports, fee registers, and spreadsheets to your device. |
| **POST_NOTIFICATIONS / FIREBASE MESSAGING** | Required to deliver critical operational push alerts, such as pending approval requests, urgent institutional notices, and security alerts. |
| **CALL_PHONE (Optional Intent)** | Used solely when an administrator manually initiates a phone call to a parent or staff member via the device dialer. |

> **Privacy Guarantee:** We never record audio in the background, track continuous GPS location, or scrape device contacts without explicit user action.

---

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We process administrative and institutional information solely for legitimate educational governance and institutional operations:
1. **Administrative Decision Making:** Providing real-time dashboard KPIs, financial oversight, student enrollment trends, and daily operational reports.
2. **Approval Workflows:** Authorizing and logging leave requests, fee concessions, payment vouchers, and student admissions.
3. **Security & Identity Verification:** Facilitating gate verification, ID scanning, and visitor management.
4. **Institutional Communication:** Broadcasting urgent notices, emergency alerts, and circulars to teachers, parents, and staff.
5. **Account Security:** Enforcing session time-outs, app lock mechanisms, and preventing unauthorized data tampering.

## 5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

**We do NOT sell, lease, trade, or monetize personal, institutional, or student data.**

Information is handled with strict confidentiality and disclosed only under these parameters:
* **With Your School Entity:** All processed records belong to your educational institution and are accessible solely by authorized personnel under institution-defined access controls.
* **Service Infrastructure Partners:** Trusted infrastructure providers (such as Google Cloud Platform and Firebase for cloud messaging and secure database hosting) process data strictly under non-disclosure and data protection compliance agreements.
* **Legal & Regulatory Obligations:** Data will be disclosed only when legally required by Nepalese law, court order, or authorized government education ministries.

---

## 6. Protection of Student & Minor Educational Records
WORKSPACE is an enterprise tool operated by adult school administrators. In processing student information:
* Data is handled strictly in accordance with educational privacy standards (including COPPA/FERPA-equivalent principles and national education data laws).
* All student information is maintained on behalf of the registered educational institution.
* We never use student information for advertising, profiling, or behavioral targeting.

---

## 7. Data Security & Storage Architecture
We enforce rigorous administrative, technical, and physical security measures:
* **End-to-End Transit Encryption:** All communication is secured via **TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3 (HTTPS)** with modern cipher suites.
* **App-Level Protection:** Client-side biometric/PIN lock mechanism to prevent unauthorized physical device access to sensitive school metrics.
* **Token Authentication:** Secure, time-limited JWT tokens with automatic revocation on logout or password reset.
* **Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Restricts data visibility strictly according to administrative hierarchy and permissions.

---

## 8. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play / App Store Compliance)

### A. Retention Period
Institutional and account data is retained for the active duration of the school's contract with Nepal School Hub or as mandated by statutory educational record-keeping guidelines.

### B. Account and Data Deletion Requests
Administrators, teachers, and guardians may request account and personal data deletion:
* **Institutional Route:** The Super Administrator of your school can deactivate or delete user accounts directly via the Nepal School Hub central administration console.
* **Direct Deletion Request:** You may submit a formal data deletion request to our Data Protection Officer at:
  * **Email:** `privacy@nshlms.com` or `support@nshlms.com`
* Upon identity verification, the personal credentials, active sessions, and direct profile data will be permanently purged within 30 days, subject only to statutory educational and financial audit requirements.
