## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Nepal School Hub - Guru (Teacher App)** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy, security, and confidentiality of school teachers, educators, administrators, students, and parents. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, store, and protect personal and sensitive user information when you use our mobile application.

This application is strictly designed for educators, teachers, and school personnel affiliated with educational institutions registered with **Nepal School Hub (NSHLMS)**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information Provided by You or Your School
To provide school management and educational services, the app processes the following information:
* **Teacher & Staff Profile:** Full name, staff ID, official email address, phone number, gender, designation, assigned classes/sections, subjects taught, and profile photograph (optional).
* **Student Academic & Educational Data:** Student full name, roll number, class/section enrollment, attendance records (Haziri), examination marks, grades, continuous assessment scores (CAS), behavioral notes, and daily diary entries.
* **Communication & Messages:** Direct messages, SMS/WhatsApp communications initiated through the portal between teachers and parents or school administration.
* **School Administration Data:** Class routines, lesson plans, leave requests, substitution allocations, and salary/payslip statements issued by the school.

### B. Device & Technical Information
* **Device Identifiers:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network connection status.
* **Application Usage Data:** Crash logs, error diagnostics, and session performance metrics to ensure app stability and reliability.

---

## 3. Device Permissions & Purpose

The app requests only permissions strictly necessary for its functionality:

| Permission | Purpose & Justification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE** | To securely communicate and synchronize academic, attendance, and grading data with your school's server via HTTPS/SSL. |
| **CAMERA** | Optional. Used only when you choose to take a profile photo or photograph student assignments, notices, or handwritten mark sheets for upload. |
| **READ / WRITE EXTERNAL STORAGE / PHOTOS** | Optional. Used to select photos, PDF lesson plans, question papers, or export student mark sheets and attendance registers to your device storage. |
| **CALL_PHONE / SMS (Optional Intent)** | Used solely when you manually tap to call or message a guardian regarding a student's emergency or attendance via standard device dialer. |

> **Note:** We never record audio, track background GPS location, or access contacts without your explicit consent.

---

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data exclusively for school operational and educational purposes:
1. **Academic Management:** Recording and calculating student attendance, examination marks, grading ledger sheets, and report cards.
2. **Classroom Administration:** Managing seating arrangements, daily teaching diaries, syllabus tracking, and lesson plans.
3. **Faculty Self-Service:** Submitting faculty leave applications, viewing class schedules, and checking authorized payroll statements.
4. **Communication:** Facilitating seamless notices, reminders, and performance updates between teachers, students, and parents.
5. **Security & Authentication:** Verifying teacher credentials via authorized JWT tokens to prevent unauthorized access to school records.

---

## 5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

**We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal or student data.** Data is strictly confidential:
* **With Your School / Educational Institution:** All student marks, attendance, and teacher records are accessible exclusively by your school’s authorized administration and the respective student/parents via the Nepal School Hub portal.
* **Service Infrastructure Providers:** We use trusted cloud hosting, database encryption, and push notification services (e.g., Firebase Cloud Messaging) strictly to operate the app infrastructure under strict data protection agreements.
* **Legal Compliance:** We may disclose data only if required by applicable Nepalese law, court order, or governmental educational authority.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy (FERPA / COPPA / Educational Records)
The Teacher App is used by adult educators and school staff. While the application processes student educational records (including minors) on behalf of the registered school:
* Such data is processed strictly as an educational service provider and processor under the direction of the school administration.
* We do not collect personal information directly from students through this app.
* Student data is protected with strict role-based access control and is never shared with third-party advertisers.

---

## 7. Data Security & Storage
We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards:
* **Encryption in Transit:** All network traffic between the mobile application and the backend API is encrypted using **TLS 1.2 / 1.3 (HTTPS)**.
* **Authentication Security:** Secure token-based authentication with automatic session timeouts.
* **Access Control:** Role-based permissions ensuring subject teachers and class in-charges access only their designated student cohorts.

---

## 8. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

### A. Data Retention
We retain user and academic data for the duration of the school's active subscription with Nepal School Hub or as required by national educational record-keeping regulations.

### B. Account & Data Deletion
Teachers and school staff have the right to request deletion of their account and associated personal data:
* **Through School Administration:** You can request your school administrator to deactivate or delete your faculty account from the Nepal School Hub Admin Portal.
* **Direct Deletion Request:** You may submit a written data deletion request to our privacy team at **privacy@nshlms.com** or **info@nshlms.com**.
* Upon verification, your account credentials, login tokens, and personal profile will be permanently deleted from active databases within 30 days, subject to mandatory educational audit retention policies required by law.
